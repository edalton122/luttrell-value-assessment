What changes for your BD team

Less admin. More selling. Every visit tied back to gross profit.

Salesforce Sales Cloud, Maps, Enablement, and Agentforce give your business development managers verified field activity, one account view, and less admin, so more of the week goes to selling and the results connect to the gross profit they are measured on.

01

Verified activity

  • Visits can't be verified
  • No proven ramp formula

Geofenced check-ins confirm visits as they happen, so the numbers line up with what is happening in the field. Route planning and territory balancing build a realistic plan from day one, and Programs let you test targets, such as how many calls or visits a week, against real results.

02

One account view

  • Deals tracked by contact
  • Unclear last touch

One company record holds every account, role, and touch: the last touch, the next steps, and the tasks. Everyone sees the whole buying committee, so it is harder for a prospect to go untouched or a follow-up to slip.

03

More selling time

  • Activity not tied to gross profit
  • Reliance on data entry

Talk, don't type: record an on-site meeting and get a transcript, a summary, and suggested next steps with Agentforce. Dashboards then connect that activity to rolling 12-month gross profit by BDM.

Assumptions and how to read this
  • The five drivers are modeled independently and added together, as in the pricing-review business case. Three raise gross profit (revenue per BDM, win rate, onboarding). Two are time savings valued at fully loaded cost (productivity, team efficiency), so no margin is applied to them.
  • Benefits ramp in at 30% of the annual amount in Year 1, 70% in Year 2, and 100% from Year 3, to allow for change management. You can edit the ramp.
  • Only win rate and productivity show a Salesforce customer benchmark (30% each) next to the modeled lift. The starting lifts (10% for win rate, 15% for productivity) are set well below that benchmark on purpose.
  • Costs reflect the Anytime licensing quote for 25 users (Sales Cloud Core Edition, Maps, Enablement, and Agentforce for Sales), with the business case rounding $105,690 a year to $105,000. Premier Success support is included. Implementation is the time-and-materials estimate from your implementation partner. Pricing is indicative and is not a quote.
  • Payback assumes each year's costs are paid up front and benefits arrive evenly through the year.
  • Demo data was synthetic. Nothing here is a measured outcome for Luttrell.
